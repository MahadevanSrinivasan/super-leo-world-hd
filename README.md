# Super Leo World HD: Dino Island

Play live at **[https://theshybulb.com/super-leo-world-hd/](https://theshybulb.com/super-leo-world-hd/)**

An original **1080p (1920×1080) 60 FPS** Mario-style platformer featuring **4 Rideable Dino Companions** inspired by *Super Mario World* and *Yoshi's Island*:

## 4 Rideable Dinos (Hatch Spotted Egg Blocks!)
- **Emerald Rex (Green Dino)**: Long Chameleon Tongue-Grab, Swallow & Spit Turbo Shells, and mid-air **Flutter-Jump** hover.
- **Blaze Rex (Red Dino)**: Spits any swallowed enemy or shell out as a **Triple Spread of Giant HD Fireballs**.
- **Sky Rex (Blue Dino)**: Feathered wings — **Multi-Flap Flight** through the sky (up to 4 mid-air wing flaps per jump).
- **Quake Rex (Yellow Dino)**: Every landing and Ground-Pound triggers an expanding **Golden Seismic Shockwave** that launches nearby enemies.

## Controls
- **Move & Wall-Slide**: `←` `→` or `A` `D`
- **Jump / Flutter-Jump / Wall-Jump**: `Space`, `W`, or `↑` (Hold in mid-air while riding a Dino to Flutter!)
- **Dino Tongue-Grab & Spit / Fireball**: `Z`, `Shift`, or `J`
- **Ground-Pound**: `↓` or `S` in mid-air
- **Dino Bail-Out Save Jump**: `E`, `X`, or `K` in mid-air
- **Mobile Phone**: Button-free direct screen gestures (`Touch: SCREEN`), gyroscope **Tilt Steering** (`Tilt: ON`), or virtual arcade gamepad (`Touch: BUTTONS`).
